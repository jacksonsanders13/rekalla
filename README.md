# Rekalla

**The daily workout for your memory.** Rekalla is a memory-practice app for
older adults who are starting to notice everyday memory slips. Think of it like
a language app — but the "lessons" are the people and routines in your own life.
A few gentle minutes a day of recall practice helps keep familiar faces and
everyday steps close.

> Rekalla is a memory-practice and brain-exercise app. It is **not** a medical
> device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is
> not a substitute for professional care.

## What you practice

- **The people who matter** — add family and friends with a photo, a name, and
  who they are to you. Rekalla quizzes you gently and revisits the ones you find
  tricky a little more often.
- **The steps of your day** — put your morning, medications, meals, and
  appointments in order and recall what comes next, built from your real routine.
- **A daily habit** — a short, achievable goal, a growing streak, and gentle
  encouragement — the simple loop that makes daily practice easy to stick with.

Family can help set up the people and routines; the daily practice belongs to
the person using it.

## Principles

- Designed for older adults — large text, high contrast, plain English.
- Gentle, never a test — there's no failing, just practice at your own pace.
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold — ever.
- Private by design — your people and routines are yours; delete everything at
  any time.
