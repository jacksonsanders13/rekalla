Common questions

What is Rekalla?

Rekalla is a daily memory-practice app. You add the people who matter and the steps of your day, and Rekalla turns them into short, gentle recall exercises — a few minutes a day to help keep familiar faces and everyday routines close.

How do I add the people and routines I want to practice?

Inside the app you add people (a photo, their name, and who they are to you) and the steps of your daily routine. Rekalla builds your practice from those. Family can help you set this up if you'd like.

Do I need to practice every day?

No — practice at whatever pace suits you. Rekalla keeps a daily streak to make it easy to build a habit, but there's no failing and no pressure. The exercises you find tricky simply come back a little more often.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Delete my account." This permanently erases your account and everything in it, and cannot be undone.
