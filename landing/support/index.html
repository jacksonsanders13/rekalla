Common questions

How does a family member connect?

The Loved One shares their 6-character connect code (shown on their Connect screen). The caregiver enters it in their own Rekalla account, and the Loved One approves the request before the caregiver gets access. A connection can be ended at any time.

Do I need a caregiver to use Rekalla?

No. When you sign up as a Loved One you can choose "I'll manage myself" and add and edit your own reminders, routine, and memory bank. You can also turn this on later in Settings.

Why didn't a reminder go off?

Reminders are notifications on your device, so make sure notifications are allowed for Rekalla (iPhone Settings → Notifications → Rekalla) and that your phone isn't on silent or Do Not Disturb. Rekalla shouldn't be your only reminder for anything critical.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Delete my account." This permanently erases your account and everything in it, and cannot be undone.
