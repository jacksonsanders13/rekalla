Questions people ask first

Will it read my handwriting?

Usually, yes, if it’s reasonably clear. Lay the page flat in good light. When it can’t read something, it says so instead of guessing.

What if it gets a date wrong?

You see everything before it’s saved, and you can change or delete anything afterwards. Nothing is ever stuck.

Who can see my calendar?

Only you. There are no shared accounts and no family dashboard. Not us either.

Do I have to type anything?

No. That’s the whole point of it. You take the photo.

Is this a medical app?

No. Rekalla is not a medical device, gives no medical advice, and will not read prescriptions or medication lists. It’s for everyday paper.

What does it cost?

Free to download and free to start. If you scan a great deal there’ll be a paid plan later, and we’ll tell you the price plainly before anything changes.

What if I don’t get on with it?

Delete the app, and delete your account from Settings. Everything in it goes too.
