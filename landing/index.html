Questions

Do I have to type anything?

No. You take a photo and check what Rekalla found. If a name or a time is wrong you can correct it, but on most documents there is nothing to change.

Will it read my handwriting?

Usually, yes, if it is reasonably clear. Lay the page flat in good light and it copes with most handwritten calendars. When it cannot read something it says so rather than guessing.

What if it gets a date wrong?

Nothing is saved until you have seen it. Everything Rekalla finds is shown to you first, and every event can be tapped and edited afterwards, or removed.

Who can see my calendar?

Only you. Rekalla is a single-person app: there are no shared accounts and nobody else is given access to what you scan. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

Is this a medical app?

No. Rekalla is not a medical device, does not give medical advice, and will not read prescriptions or medication lists. It is for everyday paper: appointments, events and bills.

What does it cost?

It is free to start, with no card needed. If you scan a great deal there will be a paid plan later, and we will tell you the price plainly before anything changes.
