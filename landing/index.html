Questions

What is Rekalla, exactly?

Rekalla is a daily memory-practice app — a bit like a language app, but the "lessons" are the people and routines in your own life. A few minutes a day of gentle recall practice helps keep familiar faces and everyday steps close.

Who is it for?

Older adults who are noticing everyday memory slips and want a simple, encouraging way to keep their recall in practice. Family can help set it up, but the daily practice is yours.

Is Rekalla free?

Rekalla is free while it's in early access. If that ever changes, we'll tell you well ahead of time.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your people, photos, and routines are yours — only you, and anyone you invite to help, can see them. No ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What devices does it work on?

Rekalla is launching on iPhone first. Support for more devices is planned.

Is Rekalla a medical app?

No. Rekalla is a memory-practice and brain-exercise app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care.
